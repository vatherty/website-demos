Waterfront Wines

A restaurant in Kelowna, British Columbia.

About the restaurant

Waterfront Wines serves wines and cocktails, with a stock of over 300 local and international wines and craft beers. The bar team makes cocktails with fresh juices and herbs, house-made syrups, and infused sugars.

The kitchen focuses on local wines and produce, with local, seasonal plates and one-off creations. The restaurant has received Gold awards for its food, including Gold at the Canadian Culinary Championships in 2012.

A private dining room seats 18 people. Reservations are recommended, particularly on weekend evenings and holidays.
