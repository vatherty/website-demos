Our story

From Singapore back to Toronto.

Kiss the Beast is the latest chapter from owners Jeanne Chai and David Burga, who returned to Toronto in 2020 after twelve years in Singapore.

Before Kiss the Beast, the pair opened Kiss My Pans — first a cheese shop, later a Singaporean restaurant — and then Agak Agak. Both businesses have since closed, and Kiss the Beast takes over the former Kiss My Pans space at 713 College Street.

The shop’s focus is Singaporean chicken rice, with Hainanese chicken rice at the centre of the menu.
