About

Fox on the Run is a lunch and coffee bar in Thunder Bay. It serves sandwiches, wraps, salads, and soups made with roasted meats, homestyle soups, and baked goods made from scratch, with Fair Trade coffees and teas. Eat in, take out, or catered.
