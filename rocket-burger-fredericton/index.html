Rocket Burger

Burgers, dogs, and more in Fredericton, New Brunswick.

About the restaurant

Rocket Burger began at the Northside Market in 2015, and opened its first full restaurant in July 2019.

It won the YFC Burger Battle in 2018 and co-won again in 2019.

The kitchen uses fresh, local meat and produce, and makes all its products daily from scratch, using real ingredients.
