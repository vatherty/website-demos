be love

A vegetarian restaurant in Victoria, British Columbia.

About the restaurant

be love serves plant-based food made from scratch. Everything served is wheat, gluten, dairy, and processed-sugar free.

Ingredients are mindfully sourced according to local and seasonal availability, from local farms and the local wilds. The menu changes with the seasons to make the most of the finest produce available.
