About

Haru Sushi is a sushi restaurant at Unit 1, 652 Flagstaff Drive in Barrhaven, Ottawa, near the corner of Borrisokane and Flagstaff Drive. It recently opened in the Half Moon Bay area and is operated by Hyoseop Kim. Dine-in and takeout are available.
