Saskatoon, Saskatchewan — Two brands under one roof

Hair, barbering and esthetics, tailored to each person.

Personalities Hair Design and Gearheads Barbershop are a family owned and operated business serving Saskatoon and area — two brands under one roof, in a comfortable, relaxing space.

About

At this studio and barbershop, each person is unique — and so is their hair. Services and your experience are tailored to your individual needs, with experienced staff supported by continuing education in current trends and techniques.

Services

Salon

Hair services from the Personalities Hair Design team, including colour, style and trims.

Barbershop

Barbering and grooming from the Gearheads Barbershop team.

Esthetics & bridal

Esthetics services and bridal services, alongside salon and barbering care.
