London barbers you can count on.

London Fades is a barbershop in the heart of London, Ontario, dedicated to men's haircuts — from fades and traditional cuts to retro styles, line ups, and beard trims. Walk in to a classic barbershop experience: a hot towel shave in a vintage, hand-upholstered leather chair, or a fresh razor line up.

About

London Fades is at 531 Colborne St, right in the heart of London, Ontario — easy to visit, with free parking, a bus stop at the front door, and minutes from downtown. Kyle Maynard is the owner and lead barber.

The shop celebrates the classic and authentic barbershop experience, and can create any look you're after.
