A Sarnia tradition since 1964.

John's Restaurant is a family-style diner serving Canadian and Greek food in Sarnia, with seating for over 200 plus a 50-seat private room, the Famous Room. All-day breakfast, lunch, dinner, family meals, and catering.

About

John's Restaurant has been a Sarnia-Lambton tradition since 1964. Much of the food is made from scratch, and the kitchen starts work at 4:00 a.m. The restaurant is known for its peameal bacon.
