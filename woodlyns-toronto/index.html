Our story

Woodlyns exists to bring people and families together around the table for one of life’s most important rituals: sharing food and drink with loved ones, creating connection, and nourishing the soul.

Woodlyns is the vision of Jeff, Nick, and Josh, blending Asian and Jewish influences into dishes deeply rooted in tradition. The kitchen focuses on exceptional ingredients, local producers, and sustainable practices whenever possible.
