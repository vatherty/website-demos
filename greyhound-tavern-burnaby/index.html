About

Greyhound Tavern soft opened on October 26, 2026, at 1908 Rosser Avenue in Brentwood, Burnaby, taking over the former Browns Socialhouse site. It is an English-style pub from Freehouse Collective, with pub games and live sports by day and weekend DJs after dark. An elevated private dining space called The Drawing Room sits above the main floor, and the streetside patio along Rosser Avenue has a heated lawn bowling lane.

The kitchen serves brasserie-style brunches and classic pub fare, including steaks, fresh-shucked oysters, and house Spaghetti Bolognese.
