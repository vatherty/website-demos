About

HELIOS is a patisserie and cake shop on Commercial Drive in Vancouver.

HELIOS is the Greek word for sun, taken from founder Jennifer Sun-Young Lee’s Korean name. The name stands for the bright, warm tone she wanted the shop to carry.

The founder

Jennifer Sun-Young Lee is a Culinary Institute of America graduate.

She has worked at the three-Michelin-star Le Bernardin in New York, Patisserie Sebastien Gaudard in Paris, and Patrice Patissier in Montreal.
