Gus's Restaurant

A family-owned restaurant on the edge of downtown Sudbury, Ontario.

About the restaurant

Gus's Restaurant was founded in 1952 by Konstantinos (Gus) Lagges. Today it is proudly owned and operated by a fourth generation of Greek owners, Spyros and Fania Koutroumanos.

The restaurant carries on Gus's tradition of great food at a reasonable price, with friendly service and a family atmosphere.
